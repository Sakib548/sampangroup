Managing Director & CEO
Md. EMAMUL HASAN
A seasoned professional with a rich tapestry of experience spanning various industries and sectors. With a distinguished track record as Managing Director across a spectrum of companies under the SAMPAN umbrella, including real estate, trade, amusement, agriculture, and more, MD Emamul Hasan brings a wealth of expertise to any role he undertakes.

His journey isn’t just limited to business ventures; he has also made significant contributions to organizational leadership and industry associations. As a former Organizing Secretary of the Bangladesh Reconditioned Vehicles Importer & Dealer Association (BARVIDA) and Joint Secretary of the Bangladesh Arm’s Dealer & Importer Association (BADIA), he has demonstrated his commitment to fostering collaboration and advancement within the business community.

Furthermore, his involvement in various clubs and associations, such as Modhumoti Club Ltd. and the Bangladesh PABX Association, underscores his dedication to community engagement and networking.

Noteworthy is his tenure as an Ex-Banker at BRAC Bank Ltd., where he garnered accolades for outstanding sales performance. His achievements, including being awarded the Sales Performance 1st Award from BRAC Bank Ltd. in 2005 and the Sales Performance High Flyer Bonanza Campaign Award from Standard Chartered Bank in 2009, attest to his exceptional prowess in driving results and exceeding expectations.


Executive Director
Major Md. Zahidul Islam (Retd)

Major Md. Zahidul Islam (Retd) is a seasoned project management professional with an extensive background in large-scale project administration and strategic leadership. Over a distinguished 30-year career in the Bangladesh Army, he gained vast expertise in project planning, engineering management, organizational development, and risk management—skills that positioned him as a trusted leader in complex and high-impact initiatives.

Throughout his service, he contributed to several nationally significant mega projects, including the Padma Multipurpose Bridge Project (PMBP), Rehabilitation of Meghna-Gumti Bridge Project, Dhaka Elevated Expressway Project, Hatirjheel Project, and the Improvement of Dhanmondi Lake Project. His involvement in these transformative infrastructures reflects his commitment to excellence and deep understanding of multidisciplinary project environments.

In addition to domestic achievements, Major Zahidul Islam brings valuable international experience. He served for two years with the United Nations (UN) in a multicultural operational environment, enhancing his global perspective and collaborative capabilities. He also worked twice with Border Guard Bangladesh (BGB), gaining experience in trans-border security, bilateral coordination, and cross-functional administration.

Known for his leadership, integrity, and dedication, he is highly skilled in team building, training, and steering organizations toward growth and efficiency.

Major Md. Zahidul Islam (Retd) has now joined Sampan Group as the Executive Director, where he brings his decades of expertise and visionary leadership to guide the organization toward innovative development and long-term success



ACADEMIC MANAGER
MD. MUHAMMAD HOQUE
Md Mokammel Hoque is a dynamic professional driven by a passion for excellence in the realms of Higher Education and Business Management & Marketing. 

With an illustrious academic background, including an MBA from the University of East London, PgD, and DET, Md Mokammel Hoque possesses a solid foundation in both Higher Education and business. 

Md Mokammel Hoque currently serves as a Teaching Fellow at Arden University, UK, bringing his expertise and passion for education to the forefront. Previously, he held the position of Lecturer at Canterbury Christ Church University, 

where he made significant contributions to the academic community. Md Mokammel Hoque's dedication to excellence in education has been recognized through his attainment of the Fellow (FHEA) designation from Advance HE, UK. 

Additionally, he holds a Professional Membership with the Chartered Institute of Management, UK, underscoring his commitment to professional development in the field. Furthermore, Md Mokammel Hoque has been honored with an Honorary Membership from OTHM Qualifications, UK, reecting his outstanding contributions to the realm of education.
